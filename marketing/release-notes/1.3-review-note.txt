iCloud sync is a one-time in-app purchase called Monthlio Pro (com.monthlio.app.pro). To reach it: open Settings (gear, top left of the Dashboard), scroll to Sync, and tap the "iCloud sync" row marked "Pro". The purchase screen shows the price and a Restore Purchases button. Sandbox and TestFlight accounts always see this row; only production accounts that downloaded the app before this version keep sync without paying. Nothing else in the app is gated. There are no user accounts or servers; data stays in the user's own iCloud. Face ID is optional and can be tried from Settings. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read a summary the app stores on the device.
