iCloud sync is a one-time in-app purchase called Monthlio Pro. In Settings, the iCloud sync row opens the purchase screen; nothing else in the app is gated. Accounts that downloaded the app before this version keep sync without paying. There are no user accounts or servers; data stays in the user's own iCloud. Face ID is optional and can be tried from Settings. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read a summary the app stores on the device.
