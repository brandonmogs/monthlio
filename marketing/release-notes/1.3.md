# Monthlio 1.3 (build 8)

## What's New (App Store, paste as plain text)

Widgets: see your next charge and your true monthly cost on the Home Screen and Lock Screen.

Backup and import: back up everything to a file, restore it on a new device, and import expenses from a CSV.

iPad: Monthlio now runs full screen on iPad, in every orientation, with keyboard shortcuts.

Monthlio Pro: iCloud sync is now a one-time purchase for new downloads. If you downloaded Monthlio before this update, sync stays free on all of your devices.

Ready for translation, plus fixes and polish throughout.

## App Review notes

iCloud sync is a one-time in-app purchase called Monthlio Pro. In Settings, the iCloud sync row opens the purchase screen; nothing else in the app is gated. Accounts that downloaded the app before this version keep sync without paying. There are no user accounts or servers; data stays in the user's own iCloud. Face ID is optional and can be tried from Settings.

## In-app purchase (create in App Store Connect before submitting)

- Product ID: com.monthlio.app.pro

- Family Sharing: on
- Display name (English): Monthlio Pro

## App Review history

- Build 6, rejected 2026-09-28: the reviewer's iPad showed a free iCloud
  sync toggle. `AppTransaction.originalAppVersion` is "1.0" in the sandbox,
  so the sandbox account was grandfathered and the purchase was unreachable.
  Fixed in build 7: only production transactions can grandfather.
- Build 7, rejected 2026-09-29 under 2.1(b): "your app failed to load
  in-app purchase products". The paywall showed "Monthlio Pro isn't
  available right now", the empty-answer path of `Product.products(for:)`.
  Cause: the Paid Apps Agreement in App Store Connect (Business >
  Agreements) was still "New". The sandbox and App Review serve no products
  until it is Active, which needs the legal entity confirmed, the agreement
  accepted by the Account Holder, and bank and tax details on file. Build 8
  retries the product request before offering "Try Again", loads the price
  concurrently with the App Store account check, and logs the App Store's
  answer under the `store` category.
- Build 8, resubmitted 2026-09-30 03:29 PDT (10:29 UTC). The agreement was
  accepted around 01:10 PDT; the sandbox catalog first returned the product
  at 03:17 PDT, about two hours later, checked by launching the app on the
  simulator without a StoreKit configuration and reading the store log.
  The API's submit call kept answering 409 "Version is not ready to be
  submitted yet" for over ten minutes, so the resubmission was made from
  the submission page's "Resubmit to App Review" button; both the version
  and the in-app purchase went to Waiting for Review.
