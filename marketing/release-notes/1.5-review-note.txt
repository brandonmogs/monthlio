Monthlio Pro (com.monthlio.app.pro, one-time purchase, already approved) unlocks iCloud sync, custom expense colors and CSV export. Reach it from Settings > Get Monthlio Pro, Settings > iCloud sync, Settings > Export CSV, or from the Color section of the expense form. Sandbox and TestFlight accounts always see the paywall. Nothing else is gated. There are no user accounts or servers; data stays in the user's own iCloud. Face ID is optional and can be tried from Settings. Notifications are optional: the per-expense notification in the expense form asks for permission when turned on.
